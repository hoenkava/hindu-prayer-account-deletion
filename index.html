Hindu Prayer - Account Deletion

Hindu Prayer respects your privacy and gives you the option to request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the Hindu Prayer application.
  2. Go to the account or profile section.
  3. Use the account deletion option if available.
  4. Alternatively, contact us using the email below.
  5. Include your registered email address and request account deletion.
Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

When your deletion request is processed, we delete the following account-associated data within our control:

  • Firebase account and user identifier
  • Google account information associated with the app
  • Kundli birth details saved in Firebase
  • Daily Journey entries saved in Firebase
  • Associated user images

Data retention

We may retain limited information when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational purposes. Any retained data will be handled according to applicable requirements.

Information stored locally on your device may remain until you clear the app data or uninstall the application. Uninstalling the app does not delete server-side data.
